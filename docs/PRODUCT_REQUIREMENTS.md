# Product Requirements

## Users

- Clinic owner
- Dentist
- Assistant
- Receptionist
- Accountant
- Patient later

## MVP Modules

- Dashboard
- Agenda
- Patients
- Patient profile
- Odontogram
- Treatment plans
- Billing
- Prescriptions
- WhatsApp communication
- Calendar integrations
- Inventory
- Reports
- Settings
- RBAC/security
- Audit logs

## Non-Negotiable MVP Features

- Patient CRUD
- Appointment calendar
- Odontogram with FDI numbering
- Tooth-level events
- Assistant draft validation
- Treatment plan from teeth
- Invoice and payment tracking
- Prescription draft/finalize
- WhatsApp provider abstraction
- Google/Outlook/ICS calendar invite abstraction

## Exclusions for MVP

- AI diagnosis
- AI radiology
- DICOM viewer
- insurance claim automation
- payroll
- full accounting
- mobile production app
