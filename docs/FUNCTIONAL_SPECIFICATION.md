# Functional Specification

## Dashboard

Show clinic KPIs, today agenda, recent patients, assistant drafts, sync health, and backup status.

## Agenda

Weekly view with appointment cards, filters, status changes, WhatsApp status, and calendar invite status.

## Patients

Create, search, filter, and view patient profiles with medical alerts and balance.

## Odontogram

Oval FDI dental chart with selected tooth drawer, diagnosis, treatment, assistant drafts, validation, notes, and timeline.

## Treatment Plans

Create plans from selected teeth with items, sessions, pricing, status, and acceptance.

## Billing

Invoices, payments, partial payments, receipt placeholder, and patient balance.

## Prescriptions

Prescription draft, allergy warning, dentist finalization, PDF placeholder.

## Communication

WhatsApp templates, message logs, delivery status, reminders.

## Calendar

Google/Outlook/ICS invitation management with privacy-safe titles.
